ABOUT CHILL & CHOW

Learn more about our restaurant, our purpose and what we want Chill & Chow to become.

OUR STORY

Chill & Chow was founded in 2023 in the township of KwaThema, located in Springs, East Gauteng.

The restaurant focuses on fast food such as chips and kotas, as well as a variety of drinks. Chill & Chow aims to provide a welcoming environment where local customers can socialise and relax.

OUR MISSION

To provide affordable, high-quality food and beverages in a welcoming environment, while creating a great Kasi spot where locals can socialise and relax.

OUR VISION

To be known as one of the best Kasi lifestyle restaurants in the local area, recognised for great food and excellent service.

OUR VALUES

QUALITY

We aim to provide high-quality food and beverages.

AFFORDABILITY

We aim to provide food that is accessible to our target customers.

COMMUNITY

We want Chill & Chow to be a place where people can connect and relax.
